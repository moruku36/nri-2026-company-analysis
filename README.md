# NRI 2026 Company Analysis

[English](README.md) | [日本語](README.ja.md)

A public-source research repository on Nomura Research Institute's management, financial performance, AI/AX strategy, governance, competition, and market evaluation. Its findings are dated snapshots, with a monthly source monitor for review candidates.

## Research approach

The recorded analysis baseline is 2026-09-25. It distinguishes disclosed results, company targets, and analytical hypotheses. It uses public information only and excludes internal company, client, and personal information.

The central hypothesis is that NRI's business knowledge, core systems, cloud/data capability, security, governance, and production operations can support its AX transition. Evaluation depends on customer outcomes, recurring revenue, margins, productivity effects, and overseas recovery.

Start with the [final assessment](08-synthesis/final-assessment.md), [strengths and risks](08-synthesis/strengths-risks.md), and [watchlist](08-synthesis/watchlist.md). These are dated research findings, not newly verified facts from this README translation.

## Monthly refresh

The Monthly NRI Source Monitor is scheduled for the first day of each month at 09:15 JST. It checks 18 sources, writes source-monitor records, and creates review issues when new primary material or important fetch failures are detected. Analysis text is updated only after evidence review.
